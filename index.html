Backtest & risk

Live: prices stream over a WebSocket from the exchange in use. When a candle closes, that timeframe is re-analysed automatically.
How it forecasts: each timeframe's last ~1,000 candles become 17 indicators (returns, EMA trend, MACD, RSI, Stochastic, Bollinger position, ATR, volume, slope, efficiency ratio, candle shape). Four models (gradient-boosted trees, logistic regression, k-nearest-neighbour pattern matching, classic technical rules) are scored with a walk-forward backtest: trained only on the past and tested on candles they never saw. The ensemble gives more weight to models that have done better. GARCH(1,1) forecasts the size of the move. "Baseline" means always guessing the more common direction. A model only has an edge if it beats that. Statistical tool, not financial advice.
